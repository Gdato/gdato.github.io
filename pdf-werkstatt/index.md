# PDF Werkstatt

Create and edit PDFs on your phone. No upload, no ads.

PDF Werkstatt is an Android app (package `com.gdato.pdfwerkstatt`). Everything it does happens on the phone itself:
there is no server behind it, so your files have nowhere to go.

## What it does today

Make a PDF from:

- **Photos** you pick from the phone.
- **Scans** of paper documents, taken with the camera.
- **Text** you type or paste.
- **Web pages**, from the address you enter (Google Play edition).
- **An ID card**: front and back on one sheet at true size, with cut lines.

Work on a PDF you already have:

- **Merge** several PDFs into one, **split** a PDF, **remove, turn, duplicate and rearrange pages**, and **crop** them.
- **Compress** it to make the file smaller, or to fit a size you set (for example at most 2 MB).
- **Passwords**: protect a PDF with a password, or remove the password from one you can open.
- **Sign** and keep your signature or stamp for next time, **mark up** a page (text, date, ticks, pen, highlights),
  and **black out** what must not be seen. **Find and black out** covers every place a word, an e-mail address, a
  phone number or an IBAN appears.
- **Fill in a form**, and mark up a paper form that is only a picture. **Make it flat** so the answers can no longer
  be changed.
- **Page numbers and stamps** on the pages.
- **Document details**: title, author, subject, keywords and language.
- **Compare** two versions of a PDF and see what changed.
- **Find** a word in a PDF.
- **Print** a PDF: the app hands it to Android's print service.
- **PDF to text**, **pages as pictures** and **the pictures inside a PDF**; **several pages on one sheet**; grey
  pictures.
- **Text recognition** makes a scan searchable. It runs on the phone, with Google's text recognition (Google Play
  edition).

## Free, all features included

The app is free, and every feature is there for everyone. There are no ads and no account. If it saved you time, you
can buy me a coffee; nobody has to.

## The promises

- **Your files never leave the phone.** The app has no server and uploads nothing.
- **No tracking by us.** We use no analytics, no advertising ID and no crash reporting. Google's components in the
  Google Play edition (text recognition, the document scanner, Google Play) are governed by Google's own terms and
  privacy policy.
- **No ads.**
- **No hidden processes.** The app works only while it is open, except that in the Google Play edition Google's
  billing library may send its own report to Google a little later.

The [privacy policy](privacy.md) says exactly what the Google Play edition does with Google Play, the document
scanner, text recognition and web pages.
