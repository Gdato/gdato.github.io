## The short version

- **No cookies.** This site stores nothing on your device, so there is no cookie banner.

## Who is responsible

applies to visitors in the European Union, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## Hosting: GitHub Pages

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P Kelly Jr St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA.
When you open a page, your browser sends a request to GitHub's servers, as with any website. According to GitHub,
it logs the IP address of every visitor for security reasons, whether or not the visitor has a GitHub account.

- **What:** your IP address, and the technical details any browser sends with a request (the page, the time, the
  browser type).
- **Why:** to deliver the pages and to keep the service secure. For the GDPR, the legal basis is a legitimate
  interest (Art. 6(1)(f)): a website cannot be shown without it.
- **Who:** GitHub. I don't receive these logs and can't see them.
- **Where:** GitHub may process the data in the USA. GitHub describes how it protects data transferred from Switzerland
  and the EU, including the Data Privacy Framework, in its
  [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- **More:** [GitHub Pages and data collection](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/about-github-pages#data-collection).

## When you email me

If you write to me, I receive your email address, your name if you give it, and your message. I use them only to
answer you and keep the conversation for as long as it is useful, then delete it. I don't share it with anyone and I
don't add you to any list. My mailbox is hosted by Google (Gmail), which processes the emails to deliver and store
them; see [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
