# Design note

The look of this site (paper and ink, one red accent, hard shadows, old-OS window bars) was inspired by
[holyarrs.com](https://holyarrs.com). No code, text, fonts or images were taken from it: the layout and the
stylesheet here were written for this site, and only system fonts are used.

The drawings (the desk, the app illustrations, the playing cards, the pixel heart and the signature) were drawn
for this site as plain SVG. The site loads nothing from other servers and runs no scripts.
