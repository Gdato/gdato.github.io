A short note

Why no ads?

Because I don't like them either. Most free apps are paid for with your attention or with your data. I would rather make something small and useful, and be able to look you in the eye.

So there is no advertising code in my apps, no analytics and no account. I run no server, so nothing about you can reach me.

If an app is useful to you, you can support it with a small donation. Nobody has to: every franc is appreciated, and none is expected.
